Welcome to Köpürö

Köpürö means «bridge». Since 2002 we have helped public associations registered under the Associations Act (1994) with the work around the work: registration, bookkeeping, grant applications and reports. We keep a directory of associations, publish the quarterly bulletin Uuk-Boo and host free pages for member associations.
