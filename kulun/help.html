Help us through the winter

From November to March the 14 horses at the Kara-Bulak pasture eat about 15 bales of hay each a month, and lucerne at Kara-Bulak station is 26 som a bale. 400 som keeps one old horse for a month. If you would like to look after one horse for the winter, write to us and we will send you its photograph and a note in the spring.

We need

  • Hay and lucerne, delivered or paid for. A farmer near Kara-Bulak can deliver; we will tell you how.
  • Old horse blankets, halters and buckets (we mend them).
  • Horseshoe nails and rasps. Farriers: we will gladly take your old rasps.
  • A trailer for one horse. Ours fell apart in August.
  • Volunteers for the open day on 14 November and for feeding on winter weekends. You do not need to know horses: we will show you.

Donations

Public Association «Kulun», Talasbank account No. 1180 0031 7705 0019. Please write «hay» or the name of the horse. Kulun receives no foreign money; everything comes from people in Irad and from our members.

Thank you to the riding school of Gymnasium No. 4, to the families of Kara-Bulak who lend us the field, and to everyone who came with a bale.
