If you or someone you know is detained

  1. Stay calm. Do not resist and do not argue on the street.
  2. Ask: «Am I being detained? On what grounds?» and ask for the officer's name and number.
  3. You may ask to call a relative or a lawyer. Give our duty number: +996-39-2-24-61-61.
  4. Do not sign anything you have not read or do not understand. You may write «I do not agree» on a record.
  5. Relatives: call the duty lawyer with the person's full name, date of birth and where and when they were taken.

This is general information, not advice for your case. Our duty lawyer will advise you.
