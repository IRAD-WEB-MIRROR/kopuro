How you can help

  • Come on a Saturday. No experience needed: we need people to write labels, carry bags and talk to neighbours.
  • Students of biology, chemistry and medicine: the TSU laboratory team needs help on the Monday after each testing day.
  • Test kits. Chlorine and turbidity tests cost us about 45 som per sample. Donations at any Talasbank branch to the account of Public Association «Taza Suu», No. 1180 0024 7735 0016 (purpose: «voluntary donation»).
  • Tell us if your water changes colour or smell. Write to tazasuu@attelkom.net with your street and district.

Thank you to Köpürö (small grant 2026-1, test kits), the TSU Faculty of Biology and Chemistry, School No. 41 and the neighbourhood committee Ak-Bulun.
