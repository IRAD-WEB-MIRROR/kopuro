Small Grants: register and follow an application

Register your application for Köpürö Small Grants here and you get its number at once. Put the number in the subject line when you e-mail the application form (2 pages) and the budget to kopuro@attelkom.net, or write it on the form if you bring it to the office. With the number you can follow the application on this page: the check of documents, the expert panel, the grant committee and the result.

Up to 25,000 som per project, for registered associations and for initiative groups with a registered partner. Paid for by Talasbank and our members' fees: not foreign funding, so no Form IA-21 is needed. See the grant notice. Questions: consultations on Tuesday and Thursday, or +996-39-2-24-17-50.

How applications are chosen

After the deadline every complete application is read by three members of the expert panel, who give marks separately; a panel member may visit the place of the project. The grant committee (the director, two board members and two representatives of member associations, with Talasbank as observer) meets about three weeks after the deadline. Projects that are not supported may apply again in the next round; the panel's comments are given at a consultation.
